About the Studio

Behind Every Beautiful Frame, There's a Story.

Founded in Hyderabad in 2020, is a boutique studio devoted to honest, emotive photography for weddings, newborns, toddlers and families.

Led by lead photographer Aarav Reddy, our philosophy is simple: never stage a feeling you can wait for. We observe, anticipate, and let real moments unfold – then edit them with a timeless, film-inspired palette.

Featured in regional wedding journals and trusted by families across Telangana and beyond.
